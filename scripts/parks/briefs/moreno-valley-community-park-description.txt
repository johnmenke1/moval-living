Moreno Valley Community Park is a 15-acre city park at 13380 Frederick Street that anchors the city's recreational core. It is the site of the city's Community Park Soccer Complex — four lit turf fields that were resurfaced in 2018 with a $2.6 million cool-fill synthetic turf investment, now used year-round by AYSO, club teams, and tournaments. Adjacent to the fields is Community Skate Park, the city's street-focused skate facility (ramps, rails, stairs, LED lighting) that the Parks & Community Services Department officially lists on its Open Play schedule.

The park also includes a children's playground (slides, swings, ladders on a wood-chip surface), four reservable picnic shelters (numbered 7-10) with grills and tables, a 1.4-mile paved walking loop around the fields, stadium lighting for evening play, public restrooms, a small concession area, and free ILoveMoVal WiFi throughout the park.

It sits directly next to the Conference and Recreation Center and City Hall along Frederick Street, making it the de facto civic-recreation hub for Moreno Valley and a regular host site for the city's Community Day of Service volunteer cleanups each spring and fall.
