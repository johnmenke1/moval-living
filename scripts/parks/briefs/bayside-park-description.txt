Bayside Park is a 2.2-acre neighborhood park in east Moreno Valley, located on Bay Avenue between Alessandro Boulevard and the Lake Perris-adjacent residential area. The park provides a playground, a reservable basketball half court, picnic areas, walking paths, green lawns, and on-site parking, making it a practical stop for local families rather than a destination-scale recreation site.

Bayside was one of the parks included in the City of Moreno Valley's historic $6 million Parks Rehabilitation & Refurbishment Program, unanimously approved by City Council in October 2022. The City specifically resurfaced the basketball court at Bayside as part of that program, alongside playground, restroom, and parking-lot upgrades across the city's 33 parks. The program completed in summer 2024.

Bayside sits about a mile north of Alessandro Boulevard in the eastern part of the city, convenient to homes along Bay Avenue and the Serrano Elementary / Creekside School area. Wildlife common to the park includes ducks, geese, and herons, and it functions primarily as a quiet, walkable neighborhood green space rather than a programmed sports complex.
