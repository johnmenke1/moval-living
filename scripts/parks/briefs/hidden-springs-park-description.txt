Hidden Springs Park is a seven-acre community park tucked at the foot of the Box Springs range, just north of Hidden Springs Elementary School. It is best known as the trailhead and gathering point for the City of Moreno Valley's signature "Hike to the Top" Hidden Springs route — a roughly 3.3-mile loop with 160 feet of elevation that the Parks & Community Services Department uses to open each year's free hike series. The park borders the 17-acre Hidden Springs Nature Park, a connected open-space preserve that gives the site its name and links it directly into the surrounding hillside trail system.

On-site amenities include a playground, picnic shelters, barbecues, a multi-use trail, and free public WiFi (City "ILoveMoVal" network) near the play structure. Oak trees shade parts of the grounds, and the park has historically been used as a staging area — including by CAL FIRE/Moreno Valley Fire for access toward the nearby "M" on Box Springs Mountain. Picnic shelters can be reserved through the City, and the park is a frequent host for MoVal beautification, painting, and trail-repair volunteer projects documented in the City Manager's monthly reports.
